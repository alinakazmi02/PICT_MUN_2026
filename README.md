# WAR™ – The World's Most Unnecessary War Portal

> *"Because apparently, talking wasn't enough."*  
> **100% Fictional. 0% Peaceful.**

A playful, interactive, humorous, satirical web application where nations declare completely unnecessary, high-drama conflicts over unreturned Tupperware, flatbread rights, tea temperature, and elevator etiquette.

---

## 🕊️ Important Disclaimer

**FICTIONAL SATIRE — CONCEPT PROJECT, NOT A REAL WAR SERVICE.**  
WAR™ is an interactive parody designed strictly for entertainment and satire. It does not facilitate, endorse, encourage, or simulate real-world warfare or political conflict. All rivalries are 100% fictional food and etiquette disputes.

---

## 🎨 Visual Aesthetic & Design

- **Palette**: Pastel pink, lavender, baby blue, mint green, canary butter yellow, and warm cream.
- **Neo-Brutalist Details**: 2.5px/3px dark borders (`#18181b`), hard-offset block shadows (`4px 4px 0px`), tilted badges (`-2deg` to `3deg`), rubber stamps ("APPROVED BY NO ONE", "ENTRY IS FREE. EXIT IS NEGOTIABLE").
- **Mascot**: **Sergeant Coo**, the tiny helmeted pigeon with blinking eyes, cartoon wobble hops, and satirical strategic advice.
- **Audio Engine**: Zero-dependency Web Audio API sound synthesizer featuring trumpet fanfares, peace chimes, cartoon boings, and pigeon coos (with an instant mute toggle).

## 🚀 Screens & Features

1. **Screen 1 – The Welcome Gate**:
   - Branding: WAR™ with animated subtitle and disclaimer badge.
   - Mascot: Sergeant Coo with interactive speech bubble.
   - Quick-launch presets (Italy, France, Canada, or Random Rogue State).
   - "ENTER THE CHAOS" and "Go Home" buttons.

2. **Screen 2 – Country Login ("Welcome, Future Aggressor")**:
   - **Full Country Dropdown**: Contains **all 193 UN Member States + 2 UN Observer States (Total 195)** in alphabetical order with flag emojis and search filter.
   - Representative name with random generator button.
   - Trivial reason for entering with pre-set grievance chips.
   - National Mood selection (Extremely offended 😤, Historically dramatic 🎭, Just bored 🥱, Resourcefully confused 🤔, Here for the plot 🍿).
   - "Remember me (history will)" checkbox with `localStorage` persistence.

3. **Screen 3 – The War Dashboard**:
   - Live country greeting: *"Welcome, [Country]. Diplomacy has left the chat."*
   - Interactive Global Chaos Meter with DEFCON levels and "Provoke Global Incident" (+5%) button.
   - Card A: Declare a Conflict.
   - Card B: Global Situation Feed with ego casualty counters and cheering.
   - Card C: Diplomatic Status Switcher (Peacefully Confused, Negotiations Pending, Mildly Offended, Dramatically Unstable, Ceasefire-ish).
   - Card D: My Country Dossier (Fictional history, defense food, and strategic perks).
   - Card E: Sergeant Coo's Strategic Bunker.

4. **Screen 4 – Declare a War ("Who Ruined Your Day?")**:
   - Opponent country selection from all 195 nations.
   - Grievance templates (Culinary Defamation, Door-Holding Insolence, Font Choice Aggression).
   - Conflict intensity levels (Mild Disagreement, Twitter-Level Drama, Internationally Awkward, Maximum Diplomatic Chaos).
   - "CONFIRM MY BAD DECISION" button with animated confetti explosion and official satirical decree.

5. **Screen 5 – Global Conflict Map**:
   - Interactive SVG World Map with clickable nation markers.
   - Animated dashed conflict trajectory arcs linking rivals.
   - Country inspector card with defense perks and direct war/peace action buttons.
   - Full status legend.

6. **Screen 6 – Diplomacy & Exit ("Department of Regrettable Peace")**:
   - Ridiculous treaty presets (The Treaty of Lukewarm Beverages, The Non-Aggression Pout Pact, The Accord of Avoided Eye Contact).
   - Insincere apology generator and ceremonial concessions.
   - "MAKE PEACE (UNFORTUNATELY)" button with peace celebration and signed Accord Certificate.

7. **The Living Room of Neutrality (Safe Zone Exit Page)**:
   - Steaming chamomile tea lounge.
   - Peaceful statistics and a button to return to the drama anytime.

8. **Extra Delights**:
   - Hall of Unnecessary Drama (Leaderboard ranking top petty nations).
   - Scrolling breaking news ticker with 15+ satirical international headlines.
   - Floating random satirical dispatch alert popups.
   - Loading animation ("Consulting the imaginary generals...").
